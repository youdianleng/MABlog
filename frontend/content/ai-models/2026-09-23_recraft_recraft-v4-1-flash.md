# English

## Summary

Recraft V4.1 Flash is a fast image generation model from Recraft, announced on September 23, 2026 as a lighter companion to Recraft V4.1. Recraft says it is the fastest model on the market, at a median of 1.3 seconds from prompt to image measured by its own team, and that it costs a fraction of V4.1, so long sessions of variations stay affordable. Recraft positions it for rapid iteration while keeping its look across photography, portraits, mood pieces, complex scenes and early concepts. The launch post compares sample outputs with other fast models such as Flux 1 Schnell, Grok Imagine Image and GPT Image 2 Low, with notes from Recraft's own creative director rather than an independent test. Recraft offers its models in Recraft Studio and through its API. Exact prices were not stated in the announcement, and no independent benchmark results for Flash were found in the sources read.

## Description

Recraft is a design-focused image platform used for illustrations, brand assets and marketing visuals. V4.1 Flash trades some of the full V4.1 model's depth for speed and price, so designers can explore many ideas quickly and switch to V4.1 for final images.

## What's new compared with the previous version

- Previous version: none in the Flash line; Flash is a faster, cheaper variant of Recraft V4.1.
- Median generation time of 1.3 seconds, according to Recraft.
- Costs a fraction of V4.1, according to Recraft (exact price not stated).

## Key capabilities

- Text-to-image generation.
- Styles from photography and portraits to complex scenes and early concepts.
- Follows exact counts in prompts in Recraft's own examples.
