# English

## Summary

Seedance 2.0 is ByteDance Seed's next-generation video creation model, officially launched on 12 February 2026. ByteDance says it supports four input modalities, text, image, audio and video, and delivers a substantial leap in generation quality, with more natural, smooth and physically plausible human motion. In MAblog's video ranking with native audio it is fourth with an Elo of 1210 ±6 in the Artificial Analysis Text-to-Video Arena, ten points behind MiniMax H3 but with a tighter confidence interval. It is the most expensive video model with a published price in the list, at about $22.45 per generated minute as measured by Artificial Analysis on 2026-10-01. It is available by subscription, through the API and in some regions only.

## Description

Seedance is ByteDance's video model line, used in its creative products and offered to developers. Version 2.0 unifies several input types in one model, so a video can be guided by text, reference images, audio and existing video at the same time.

## What's new compared with the previous version

- Supports four input modalities: text, image, audio and video (ByteDance).
- Improved naturalness and physical plausibility of human motion (ByteDance).

## Key capabilities

- Text, image, audio and video input.
- Video generation with native audio.
