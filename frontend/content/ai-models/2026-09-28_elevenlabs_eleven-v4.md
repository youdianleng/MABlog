# English

## Summary

Eleven v4 is ElevenLabs' newest text-to-speech model, launched on September 28, 2026 together with a low-latency variant, Eleven v4 Turbo. ElevenLabs calls it its most emotive model yet, built on an entirely new architecture to interpret tone, pacing, emotion, character and context, and says it is ranked first by Artificial Analysis and preferred by about 75% of listeners in blind head-to-head tests. Users can direct delivery in natural language or with inline audio tags such as [laughs], and multi-speaker dialogue responds to the context of a whole scene. Both models support more than 90 languages, a voice recorded in one language can speak others with a native accent, and Instant Voice Clones need about 10 seconds of audio. Eleven v4 Turbo targets voice agents, with a median time to first speech of about 150 ms. The models are offered in the ElevenLabs apps and through its API; prices were not stated in the announcement.

## Description

ElevenLabs is a voice AI company whose models power narration, dubbing, games and voice agents. Eleven v4 is its new flagship for expressive speech, while the Turbo variant brings the same architecture to low-latency agents. Better speaker similarity and accent adherence target dubbing and localisation work in particular.

## What's new compared with the previous version

- Previous version: not named in the announcement, which compares it with earlier generations of text-to-speech models.
- Entirely new architecture with higher audio fidelity, according to ElevenLabs.
- Follows audio tags and direction prompts more accurately; improved IPA phoneme support.
- More consistent multi-speaker dialogue and stronger accent adherence across languages.
- Instant Voice Clones from about 10 seconds of audio.

## Key capabilities

- Text-to-speech in more than 90 languages.
- Natural-language direction and inline audio tags, including sound effects.
- Cross-language voice cloning with native accents.
- Low-latency Turbo variant with about 150 ms median time to first speech.
