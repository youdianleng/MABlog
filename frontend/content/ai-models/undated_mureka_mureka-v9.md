# English

## Summary

Mureka V9 is Mureka's full-song music model, offered free, by subscription and through an API. In MAblog's music rankings it leads the instrumental leaderboard with an Elo of 1177 ±17 in the Artificial Analysis Music Arena (Instrumental), six points ahead of Suno V5.5 with overlapping ranges, which makes it effectively a shared lead. For vocal songs it is second at 1136 ±16, 24 points behind Suno but 44 points clear of third place. Combined with its API access, this makes Mureka V9 the most balanced music option for developers who need both vocal and instrumental output. Mureka's website loads its content by script and has no news page, so the release date, plan prices and API price could not be confirmed for this file.
