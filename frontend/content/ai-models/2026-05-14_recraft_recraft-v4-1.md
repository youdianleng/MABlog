# English

## Summary

Recraft V4.1 is Recraft's image generation model announced on May 14, 2026, the current main model of the Recraft line that Recraft V4.1 Flash later joined as its fast variant. Recraft says V4.1 makes images feel more alive and natural: photorealism is quieter and more realistic, with fewer random background details, new illustration styles become possible, and the model gets the aesthetic right from short prompts instead of very long instructions. Recraft's launch post compares its results with Midjourney and GPT Image 2 on people, interiors, lettering and vector-style logos, judged by Recraft itself. Alongside the main model, Recraft added a Utility variant with flat lighting, front-facing composition and simple scenes for mockups and product shots. Users can create with V4.1 in Recraft Studio, which can be started for free, or through the Recraft API. Prices were not stated in the announcement.

## Description

Recraft is a design-focused image platform used for illustration, branding, vector art and marketing visuals. V4.1 is its expressive main model, which experiments with light, angle and composition; the Utility variant gives predictable results, and the later Flash variant trades depth for speed.

## What's new compared with the previous version

- Previous version: Recraft V4.
- Quieter, more realistic photorealism with fewer stray background details, according to Recraft.
- New illustration styles and stronger lettering and vector work.
- Better results from short prompts.
- New Utility variant for simple, predictable images.

## Key capabilities

- Text-to-image generation from photorealism to illustration and vector-style art.
- Lettering and logo work.
- Utility variant for mockups and product shots.
