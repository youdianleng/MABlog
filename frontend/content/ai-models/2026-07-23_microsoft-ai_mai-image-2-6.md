# English

## Summary

MAI-Image-2.6 is Microsoft AI's flagship image model, published on 23 July 2026 and built, in Microsoft's words, around how people create, with precise control over every edit. It produces design-ready images from text, images, web context and visual references, with control over composition, style and detail, and can be tried in Microsoft's playground. In MAblog's image ranking it is third with an Elo of 1147 ±10 in the Artificial Analysis Text-to-Image Arena, inside the confidence interval of second place, so the gap is not meaningful. It costs about a third less than Grok Imagine Image 2.0, at roughly $38.90 per 1,000 images at 1024×1024 as measured by Artificial Analysis on 2026-10-01. Microsoft also offers MAI-Image-2.6-Flash at less than half the flagship's price.

## Description

MAI-Image-2.6 is part of Microsoft's in-house MAI model family. It focuses on controllable generation and editing for both casual and professional use. The lighter MAI-Image-2.6-Flash trades some quality for cost, which Microsoft describes as production-ready quality at less than half the price of the flagship.
