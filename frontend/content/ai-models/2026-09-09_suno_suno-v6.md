# English

## Summary

Suno v6 is Suno's new generation of music models, announced on September 9, 2026 and developed with industry partners including Warner Music Group, BMG and Believe. It comes as three models: v6, the flagship for Pro and Premier subscribers, aimed at reliable, polished songs in any genre; v6-wild, also for Pro and Premier, which is deliberately less predictable for exploring ideas; and v6-mini, a faster, more efficient version available to everyone, including free users. Suno says all three understand more of the language musicians use, from vocals and instrumentation to structure, mood and references. New tools let creators edit part of a song in plain language, build mashups from several sources, sample and isolate parts, change a single lyric and start from text, audio, images or video. Suno plans to retire its previous models and move entirely to the v6 generation. Plan prices were not stated in the announcement.

## Description

Suno is a consumer music-creation platform. v6 is its first suite of models aimed at different kinds of creators rather than a single model, and the first developed with major music-industry partners. Suno also describes new safeguards that screen uploaded audio and lyrics for unauthorised use.

## What's new compared with the previous version

- Previous version: Suno v5.5.
- Three models instead of one: v6, v6-wild and v6-mini.
- Developed with Warner Music Group, BMG and Believe.
- Plain-language editing of song sections and single lyrics, mashups, and sampling with isolation.
- Multimodal starting points: text, audio, images and video.

## Key capabilities

- Full songs with vocals and instrumentation in any genre.
- Edit part of a song while keeping the rest.
- Create from text, voice memos, images or video.
- Sample, isolate and build beats in one workflow.
