# English

## Summary

Suno V5.5 is Suno's song generation model, released on 26 March 2026 together with Voices, custom models and the My Taste feature. In MAblog's music rankings it is the clear leader for vocal songs, with an Elo of 1160 ±14 in the Artificial Analysis Music Arena (Vocals) and the only undisputed source rank of 1, and it is statistically level with first place for instrumental music at 1171 ±15. Suno is a consumer product with free access and subscriptions; no API access is listed, so no comparable API price is shown. For people who want complete songs with convincing vocals from a text prompt, it is the strongest choice in this edition. Suno has since released a newer v6 model generation, which is not part of this ranking.
