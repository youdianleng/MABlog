# English

## Summary

Kling 3.0 is Kuaishou's video generation model, offered by subscription in the Kling apps and to developers through the Kling API. In MAblog's video ranking with native audio it holds fifth place with an Elo of 1095 ±6 in the Artificial Analysis Text-to-Video Arena, after a large drop from fourth place and a source rank range of 10–13. It ties SkyReels V4 on Elo, and MAblog's editors gave Kling the last slot on reliability evidence. Its comparable price is about $10.08 per generated minute for the 1080p Pro tier, as measured by Artificial Analysis on 2026-10-01. Kling's official documentation loads its content by script, so details such as the release date, durations and plan prices could not be confirmed for this file and are marked as not published.
