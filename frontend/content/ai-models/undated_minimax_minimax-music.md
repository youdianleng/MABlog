# English

## Summary

MiniMax Music is the music generation model from MiniMax, available free, by subscription and through the MiniMax API. In MAblog's music rankings it is fourth for vocal songs with an Elo of 1084 ±13 in the Artificial Analysis Music Arena (Vocals), within the confidence intervals of both third and fifth place, and fifth for instrumental music at 1063 ±13, 39 points below fourth place. MiniMax publishes its music models alongside its video, speech and language models, so developers already using the MiniMax API can add music generation with the same account. The official link in MAblog's snapshot points to the MiniMax Music 2.6 announcement from April 2026, while MiniMax now lists Music 3.0 as its current model, so the exact ranked version is being confirmed.

## Description

MiniMax Music is part of MiniMax's broad media model lineup, which also includes the H3 video model and MiniMax Speech. It covers both vocal songs and instrumental tracks, and is offered in MiniMax's own products as well as through its API.
